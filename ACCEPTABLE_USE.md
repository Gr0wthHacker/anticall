# Acceptable Use Policy

> **DRAFT - service/network rules, not extra restrictions inserted into the AGPL. NOT LEGAL ADVICE; attorney review required.**

This policy governs maintainer-operated hosted services, public federation services, community infrastructure, and support channels, to the extent stated in applicable service terms. Independent use of the licensed source code remains governed by the software license ([`LICENSE`](LICENSE)) and applicable law. No such services exist today.

## Permitted intended behavior

- Inbound screening of lines you control
- Consensual call transfers
- Individually authorized, legitimate opt-out requests to **verified** official contact channels
- Recordkeeping and evidence export
- Complaint preparation (user-reviewed)
- Privacy-preserving defensive signals

## Prohibited on maintainer-operated services

- Mass or coordinated retaliatory calling
- Repeated calls intended to harass or overwhelm
- Calls to unverified or spoofed-number owners
- Doxxing third parties
- Impersonating officials or regulators
- Threats
- Unlawful recording
- Data theft
- Evading provider rules or throttling
- Using community data as a target list
- Fabricated complaints
- Operating someone else's line without authorization

## Enforcement

Rate limits, case review, account or network suspension, artifact deletion, and referral to providers or authorities where necessary and lawful. A documented appeal and correction process will be maintained. The project does not guarantee monitoring of independently deployed instances and does not supervise self-hosters.

## Reporting abuse

Open an issue with the abuse-report template, or a private security advisory for sensitive detail. See [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).
